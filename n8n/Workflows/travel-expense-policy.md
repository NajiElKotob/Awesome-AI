# Travel & Expense Policy

**Document code:** FIN-POL-007
**Version:** 3.2
**Owner:** Finance Department
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors and interns travelling or spending on behalf of the organization

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how employees plan business travel, which costs the organization pays for, and how to claim them back. Its goals are to keep employees safe and comfortable while travelling, to spend the organization's money responsibly, and to make claims simple and fair for everyone.

The policy covers:

- Business trips (local and international)
- Daily work-related expenses (client meals, taxis, mileage, small purchases)
- Corporate cards and cash advances
- Claims, approvals and audits

It does **not** cover relocation, training fees or personal travel. These are handled by separate policies.

All amounts in this policy are stated in **USD**. Employees paying in another currency are reimbursed at the organization's monthly exchange rate published by Finance on the first working day of each month.

---

## 2. Key Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Business trip** | Travel outside the employee's base city for work, lasting at least one overnight stay or more than 100 km one way. |
| **Base city** | The city where the employee's main office is located. |
| **Traveller** | The employee taking the business trip. |
| **Approver** | The traveller's direct manager, unless a higher level is required (see Section 4). |
| **Per diem** | A fixed daily allowance for meals and small incidental costs. |
| **City tier** | A cost level (A, B or C) used to set hotel caps and per diem rates. See Section 6. |
| **Travel Desk** | The internal team (or approved agency) that books flights and hotels. |
| **Claim** | A request to be reimbursed for expenses paid personally. |

---

## 3. General Principles

1. **Spend as if it were your own money.** Choose reasonable, cost-effective options that still allow you to work safely and effectively.
2. **Book early.** Flights must be booked at least **14 days** before departure where possible. Bookings made less than 7 days before departure require a short written reason.
3. **Use the Travel Desk.** All flights and hotels must be booked through the Travel Desk. Self-booked travel is reimbursed only if it is cheaper than the Travel Desk quote and has been approved in advance.
4. **No personal gain.** Loyalty points and miles earned on business travel may be kept by the employee, but they must never influence the choice of airline or hotel when a cheaper equivalent option exists.
5. **Honesty.** Claims must be true, complete and supported by receipts. False claims are treated as a serious disciplinary matter (see Section 13).

---

## 4. Trip Approval

All business trips must be approved **before** any booking is made, using the Travel Request Form.

### 4.1 Approval levels

| Trip type | Estimated total cost | Required approval |
|---|---|---|
| Local trip | Up to USD 1,500 | Direct manager |
| International trip | Up to USD 5,000 | Direct manager + Department Head |
| Any trip | USD 5,001 – 15,000 | Department Head + Finance Director |
| Any trip | Above USD 15,000 | Chief Executive Officer |

### 4.2 Special cases

- **Trips longer than 21 days** always require Department Head approval, whatever the cost.
- **Group travel** (four or more employees on the same trip) must be approved by the Department Head so that bookings can be combined and costs reduced.
- **Travel to high-risk destinations** (as listed by the Security Team) requires written approval from the Security Team in addition to the normal approvals.
- **Interns** may travel only when accompanied by, or approved by, a manager.

## 5. Air Travel

### 5.1 Class of travel

| Flight duration (scheduled, one way) | Allowed class |
|---|---|
| Under 6 hours | Economy |
| 6 to 9 hours | Premium Economy |
| More than 9 hours | Business class – Director level and above only; all others Premium Economy |

- Flight duration means scheduled time in the air, **not** including layovers.
- Executives (C-level) may travel Business class on any flight longer than **4 hours**.
- Employees may upgrade at their own cost, or using their own miles, at any time.

### 5.2 Booking rules

- Choose the **lowest logical fare**: the cheapest available flight within a **2-hour window** of the preferred departure time, with no more than **one stop**.
- Fully refundable tickets are allowed only when plans are likely to change and the price difference is **less than 20%** of the non-refundable fare.
- Checked baggage is reimbursed for one standard bag. Extra bags are reimbursed only when carrying work equipment or materials.
- Seat selection fees are reimbursed up to **USD 50** per flight.
- Airport lounge access is not reimbursed, except for layovers longer than **5 hours**, capped at **USD 60** per visit.

### 5.3 Changes and cancellations

If a trip is cancelled or changed for business reasons, the organization pays the change fees. If it is changed for personal reasons, the traveller pays any extra cost. Unused tickets must be reported to the Travel Desk within **5 working days** so that the credit can be reused.

## 6. Accommodation

### 6.1 City tiers

Destinations are grouped into three tiers based on the cost of living. Finance publishes the full city list every January.

| Tier | Description | Example profile |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier A** | Very high cost | Major global financial capitals |
| **Tier B** | Medium cost | Large regional cities and national capitals |
| **Tier C** | Lower cost | Smaller cities and towns |

If a city is not on the list, it is treated as **Tier B**.

### 6.2 Hotel caps (per night, including taxes)

| Tier | Maximum room rate |
|---|---|
| Tier A | USD 280 |
| Tier B | USD 180 |
| Tier C | USD 120 |

- Hotels should be **3 or 4 stars** and within **30 minutes** of the meeting location.
- Rates up to **15% above the cap** are allowed during major events or peak seasons, with a written explanation.
- Stays longer than **14 nights** in one location should use a serviced apartment if it is cheaper.
- Staying with friends or family instead of a hotel is allowed. In that case, the traveller may claim a **USD 30 per night** private accommodation allowance (no receipt needed).

### 6.3 Hotel extras

- Hotel internet (Wi-Fi) is reimbursed if not included in the room rate.
- Laundry is reimbursed only for trips longer than **5 nights**, up to **USD 25 per night** after the fifth night.
- Minibar, movies, spa, gym and room upgrades are **not** reimbursed.
- No-show charges are the traveller's responsibility unless caused by a business reason (for example, a delayed flight).

## 7. Meals and Per Diem

### 7.1 Daily per diem rates

Travellers receive a fixed per diem for each full day of a business trip. It covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, tips and small incidental costs. **No receipts are needed** for the per diem.

| Tier | Full-day per diem |
|---|---|
| Tier A | USD 90 |
| Tier B | USD 65 |
| Tier C | USD 45 |

### 7.2 Partial days and deductions

- **Travel days** (the day of departure and the day of return) are paid at **75%** of the full rate.
- If a meal is already provided (by the hotel, a conference, a client or on a long flight), deduct from the daily rate as follows:
  - Breakfast: **15%**
  - Lunch: **30%**
  - Dinner: **40%**
- Trips with no overnight stay receive a flat **USD 25** meal allowance if the traveller is away for more than **10 hours**.

### 7.3 Example

A traveller spends 4 days in a Tier B city: arrives Monday, leaves Thursday. Breakfast is included at the hotel on Tuesday and Wednesday.

- Monday (travel day): 65 × 75% = USD 48.75
- Tuesday: 65 − 15% (breakfast) = USD 55.25
- Wednesday: 65 − 15% (breakfast) = USD 55.25
- Thursday (travel day): 65 × 75% = USD 48.75
- **Total per diem: USD 208.00**

## 8. Ground Transport

### 8.1 Taxis and ride-hailing

Taxis and ride-hailing apps are allowed for airport transfers, travel between meetings, and when public transport is unsafe, unavailable or would waste significant working time. Standard car types only – premium or luxury car options are not reimbursed.

Late-night taxis home are reimbursed when an employee works past **9:00 PM** in their base city, up to **USD 40** per trip.

### 8.2 Car rental

- Rental cars are allowed only when they are cheaper than taxis or when travelling to locations without good transport options.
- Book an **economy or compact** car. A larger car is allowed for **3 or more** travellers sharing the car, or when carrying equipment.
- Fuel is reimbursed at cost. Prepaid fuel options are not reimbursed.
- Full insurance (collision damage waiver and third-party liability) must always be accepted.
- Traffic fines and parking fines are the driver's responsibility and are **never** reimbursed.

### 8.3 Personal car (mileage)

Employees using their own car for business are paid **USD 0.45 per kilometre**. Normal commuting between home and the base office is not included. Parking and road tolls are reimbursed on top of the mileage rate, with receipts.

### 8.4 Trains

Train travel is encouraged for trips under **4 hours** if it is cheaper or similar in cost to flying. Standard class is allowed; first class is allowed for journeys longer than **3 hours**.

## 9. Client Entertainment and Business Meals

Business meals with clients, partners or prospects are allowed when they have a clear business purpose.

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Maximum cost per person (including the employee) | USD 75 |
| Alcohol | Up to 25% of the total bill; never reimbursed in countries or venues where it is not permitted |
| Internal team meals (employees only) | USD 30 per person, maximum 4 times per year per team, with Department Head approval |
| Gifts to clients | Up to USD 100 per client per year; gifts above USD 50 must be recorded in the Gifts Register |

For every business meal, the claim must list:

1. The names and organizations of all attendees
2. The business purpose of the meal
3. An itemised receipt (not only the card slip)

The **most senior employee** present must pay for the meal and submit the claim.

Entertainment for government officials requires prior approval from the Compliance Team, whatever the amount.

## 10. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following costs will **not** be reimbursed:

- Personal entertainment (cinema, sightseeing, sports events) unless part of approved client entertainment
- Minibar, room service movies, spa, gym and hotel upgrades
- Traffic, parking and speeding fines
- Lost or stolen personal items, including luggage contents
- Passport renewal fees (visa fees for business trips **are** reimbursed)
- Travel costs for family members or companions
- Excess baggage for personal items
- Personal grooming, clothing and toiletries (unless luggage is delayed more than **24 hours**, then up to **USD 100** for essentials)
- Late payment fees and interest on personal credit cards
- Charitable donations made in the organization's name without Finance approval

## 11. Payment Methods

### 11.1 Corporate card

Employees who travel more than **4 times per year** receive a corporate card.

- The corporate card must be used for **business expenses only**. Personal use is not allowed, even if repaid later.
- Monthly card limit: **USD 5,000** (can be raised temporarily with Finance Director approval).
- Card transactions must be reconciled in the expense system within **10 days** of the statement date.
- Lost or stolen cards must be reported to the card provider and to Finance within **24 hours**.

### 11.2 Cash advances

Employees without a corporate card may request a cash advance for trips longer than **3 days**.

- Maximum advance: **80%** of the estimated trip cost, up to **USD 3,000**.
- Requests must be submitted at least **5 working days** before departure.
- Any unused amount must be returned within **10 working days** of the trip ending.
- A new advance cannot be issued while a previous one is still open.

### 11.3 Personal payment

Employees may pay with personal funds and claim reimbursement. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll if the claim is approved before the **20th** of the month.

---

## 12. Submitting Claims

### 12.1 Deadlines

- Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the trip end date or the expense date.
- Claims submitted between **31 and 90 days** late require Department Head approval.
- Claims older than **90 days** will **not** be reimbursed.
- Claims for the final month of the financial year must be submitted within **10 days** of year-end.

### 12.2 Receipts

- Original or clear digital receipts are required for every expense of **USD 25 or more**.
- Expenses under USD 25 may be claimed without a receipt, up to a total of **USD 75 per trip**.
- A lost receipt may be replaced by a signed Missing Receipt Declaration, maximum **twice per year** per employee.
- Receipts must show the date, vendor name, amount, currency and what was purchased.

### 12.3 Review process

1. The employee submits the claim in the expense system with receipts attached.
2. The approver reviews it within **5 working days**.
3. Finance checks it against this policy within **5 working days** of approval.
4. Approved claims are paid with the next payroll (see Section 11.3).

An approver may never approve their own expenses, or expenses for a trip they took part in as the most senior person. In such cases the claim goes to the next level up.

---

## 13. Audits and Policy Violations

Finance audits a random sample of at least **10%** of all claims every quarter, plus all claims above **USD 10,000**.

| Violation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Minor mistake (wrong category, small calculation error) | Claim returned for correction |
| Spending above limits without approval | Amount above the limit not reimbursed |
| Repeated violations (3 or more in 12 months) | Written warning and loss of corporate card for 6 months |
| False or inflated claims | Full repayment and disciplinary action, which may include termination |

Employees who notice suspected misuse can report it confidentially through the organization's whistleblowing channel.

---

## 14. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be requested **in writing before** the expense, with a clear business reason. They need approval from the Department Head **and** the Finance Director. Exceptions approved after the expense has been made are allowed only in genuine emergencies (for example, medical issues, natural disasters or security incidents).

---

## 15. Traveller Safety and Insurance

- All employees on business trips are covered by the organization's **business travel insurance** from the moment they leave home until they return. This covers emergency medical costs, evacuation, trip cancellation and lost baggage.
- Travellers must register every trip with the Travel Desk so they can be reached in an emergency.
- In an emergency, the traveller should first ensure their own safety, then call the **24/7 travel assistance number** printed on the back of their travel insurance card.
- Employees may refuse travel to a destination they believe is unsafe without any negative consequence, and should discuss this with their manager and the Security Team.

---

## 16. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed **every year** by the Finance Department. Rates for hotels, per diem and mileage may be updated at any time, and changes take effect from the first day of the following month. Questions about this policy should be sent to the Finance Department.
